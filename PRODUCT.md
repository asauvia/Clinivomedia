## Platform

## Stack

delegated: one self-contained static HTML/CSS/JS page (no build step), so it can be published as a shareable page today and dropped onto any host later.

## Users

Doctors, clinic owners and hospital admins in India (inferred from the Clinivo deck). They are busy practitioners who know medicine, not marketing; they land on the site from Instagram (@clinivomedia), WhatsApp or a referral and decide whether Clinivo is credible enough to call.

## Product Purpose

Clinivo Media builds and runs the digital presence of medical practices: websites, Instagram content, Google Business Profile and reviews, plus growth consulting. Success: a doctor understands the offer in one viewport and calls, WhatsApps or emails Deeptanshu.

## Positioning

Healthcare-only. Treats the clinic as a business and patients as people. Ethical, patient-first content that respects Indian medical advertising rules; honest expectations, no inflated promises. One partner for website, social and Google.

- GrowthDoc: website, social, Google, reviews, personal branding and broader digital management.

- Process: Consultation, Strategy, Creation, Launch, Management, Growth.
